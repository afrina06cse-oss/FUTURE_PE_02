# Prompt Output Samples

## Sample 1 - UGC Hook Generation

### Prompt Used

Generate 10 short, authentic UGC-style hooks for GlowNest Hydrating Face Serum. The hooks should feel conversational, relatable, and suitable for short-form social media videos.

### Sample Output

1. My skincare routine used to feel way more complicated than it needed to be.
2. If your skin feels dry after a long day, try this simple step.
3. One small addition changed how simple my skincare routine feels.
4. I wanted hydration without adding five more products to my routine.
5. POV: You finally find a skincare product that fits your busy routine.

## Sample 2 - UGC Advertisement

### Prompt Used

Create a short UGC-style advertisement for GlowNest Hydrating Face Serum using a Problem-Solution-CTA structure. Keep the tone natural and conversational.

### Sample Output

Hook:
"My skincare routine used to feel way more complicated than it needed to be."

Problem:
"I was using too many products and still wanted a simple way to keep my skin feeling hydrated."

Solution:
"Then I added GlowNest Hydrating Face Serum to my routine. It's lightweight and easy to add to my everyday skincare steps."

CTA:
"If you're looking for a simple hydration step, check out GlowNest."

## Sample 3 - Platform Adaptation

### Prompt Used

Adapt the GlowNest Hydrating Face Serum UGC advertisement for Instagram Reels, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.

### Sample Output

### Instagram Reels

Use a quick opening hook, casual talking-to-camera style, short sentences, and a clear CTA.

### Facebook

Focus on the relatable skincare problem, explain the product clearly, and finish with a direct CTA.

### YouTube Shorts

Use a quick hook, short conversational sentences, a simple product explanation, and a strong closing CTA.

## Prompt Engineering Approach

The prompts were designed using clear instructions, defined target audience, product context, desired tone, content structure, and platform requirements.

This helped generate consistent and reusable UGC marketing content for the skincare brand.
